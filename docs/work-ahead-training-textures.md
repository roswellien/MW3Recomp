# MW3 Training texture proof of concept

Status: **training terrain proof of concept prepared; complete mission texture coverage still in progress**. The scope is the four Training missions represented by `MW3/zbd/t1/readerm1.zbd` through `readerm4.zbd`. The user requested a non-effects game-texture pass.

The local contact-sheet review (not included in this public documentation snapshot) and inventory JSON (not included in this public documentation snapshot) cover the three raster banks `rtexture.zbd`, `rtexture2.zbd`, and `rtexture3.zbd`. They contain the same **385 distinct decodable names** across different size variants; the largest available variant is recorded for each name. Current strict decoding accepts 385 entries per bank and rejects 21 per bank.

A readable-name screen marks **116 effect-like textures** for exclusion and leaves **269 non-effect candidates**. This is an archive-level candidate count, not a verified mission-use count. It includes terrain, structure, mech, vehicle, cockpit and other artwork. **290 texture names** occur in the T1 `gamez.zbd` string table, useful as a cross-reference but insufficient alone to prove that a texture is rendered in a given mission. The four mission reader files identify actors, regions and mission-specific names, but exact geometry/material-to-texture bindings remain open. `texture.zbd`, `texture1.zbd` and `texture2.zbd` use a separate compressed encoding not supported by the strict project decoder; their needed T1 assets have now been extracted with Mech3AX for the GameZ-linked terrain pass.

A 14-texture terrain/water proof of concept (not included in this public documentation snapshot) now contains full-AI drafts, exact 4× exports and editable documents. Three source IDs (`cratrt1`, `cratrt1a`, `ggter1`) are explicitly named in the training reader configuration; remaining terrain entries are candidates pending exact material linkage.

The Training next-pass v2 review (not included in this public documentation snapshot) bundles the two Bushwacker shared-material gap drafts (`footpad`, `weap_hole`), the earlier 14 terrain/water candidates, and seven additional textures directly linked by T1 GameZ materials. The seven new candidates (`beach02a`, `dkmudmesa`, `ltmudmesa`, `tertrans1`, `tertrans2`, `tertrans4`, `tertrans5`) have exact 4x exports, preserved source alpha, editable OpenRaster files, export reports, and source hashes. See the 19-name GameZ crosswalk (not included in this public documentation snapshot). The first 14 candidates were decoded from `rtexture.zbd`; twelve same-name assets also occur in GameZ but their decoded pixels differ from the separate `texture.zbd` source bank. Keep their provenance separate until runtime bank selection is confirmed. `t1ocean` and `watrock01` are rtexture-only candidates. Reader identifiers `qsnd01..qsnd07` and `trench` remain unmapped to exact materials. The first test replaced only the GameZ-linked `texture*` banks; the user saw the mech AI but no new terrain in Training Mission 1. The corrected test now also replaces the T1 reader-linked `rtexture*` family with all 14 prepared terrain candidates, including `cratrt1`, `cratrt1a`, and `ggter1` named by the readers. All 14 are installed across `rtexture.zbd`, `rtexture2.zbd`, and `rtexture3.zbd`, with their 4×-of-source forms. All 392 non-target images per `rtexture*` archive and all 399 non-target images per `texture*` archive are confirmed pixel-identical. The seven GameZ-linked drafts remain in the separate `texture*` family. Both source families are staged only in the disposable game test. Visual rendering and seams still need a Mission 1 pass. All launch scripts are now in `local/work-ahead/training-game-launchers/`. Mission 1 instructor skin ID 7 is known, but its letter-suffix mapping remains unresolved.

The mission readers also name the Bushwacker in training missions 1–3 and a Firefly actor in mission 4. The global `rmechtex` table has 120 named Bushwacker atlas records and 112 Firefly atlas records, including multiple letter-suffixed variants; that is a family inventory, not a proven count of variants loaded in Training. Existing reviewed AI work for `bushwacker01`, `bushwacker04` and `firefly01` may be reusable after exact source hashes are matched.

Next work is to review the two shared-material gap drafts, validate them in the
disposable Bushwacker Training copy, and confirm mission-1 terrain/material
bindings for the starter terrain set. Then continue with confirmed terrain,
vehicle, cockpit, and structure textures and resolve the compressed texture
banks. Keep uncertain assets in a candidate list; do not label all 269 as

## Training mech texture pass

The Training-specific mech atlas review (not included in this public documentation snapshot) and offline package (not included in this public documentation snapshot) cover all 29 unsuffixed atlas slots for the Bushwacker (15) and Firefly (14) named by the mission readers. Three previously reviewed AI exports are reused by exact source identity. The other 26 have full-AI RGB exports, source-alpha preservation, export reports, and editable OpenRaster files. All 29 base slots have exact 16× source dimensions, and the user approved the current previews after the v2 corrections. These remain work-ahead candidates, not game-integrated replacements.

Eight extreme-ratio atlases were prepared from ratio-compatible tiles and assembled at their original tile boundaries without cropping or stretching. The source roster also inventories 203 lettered alternate-skin records; they are not confirmed Training requirements until the reader skin settings and suffix mapping are resolved. The name-to-global-material-index mapping for all 29 base atlases is recorded in `local/work-ahead/training-mech-textures-v1/mechlib-material-map.json`.

Visual review corrections approved by the user: Bushwacker 07 now uses overlapping source-context tiles blended across joins. Firefly 08, 09, 10, 13, and 14 were regenerated using their Firefly source tiles alone, without the Bushwacker 01 style reference; Firefly 13 reuses Firefly 08 tile results only where source tile hashes match. The review keeps links to the v1 drafts for comparison.

Mission-reader inspection confirms Bushwacker actors in Training missions 1–4 and a Firefly actor in mission 4. A bounded parser now reads polygon material IDs from the four corresponding mechlib models and resolves their 55 used material indices: 54 unique textured materials and one untextured material. The 54 textures comprise the 29 approved base atlases, 22 damage names whose source pixels exactly match an approved atlas, and three distinct shared mech textures (`mech01`, `weap_hole`, `footpad`) with AI drafts awaiting review; the `weap_hole` v1 black outer field is superseded by a v2 candidate, and the current `footpad` AI draft has a layout mismatch. Per-model usage, polygon counts, source identities, and package paths are recorded in `local/work-ahead/training-mech-textures-v1/mechlib-model-material-usage.json` and `required-texture-roster.json`; the supplemental review is `mech-usage-review.html`. Polygon material and UV links are now verified for all 54 textured assets. All have UV-bearing polygons; 670 stored UV components exceed [0,1], so renderer wrap behavior still needs confirmation. The UV bounds are available in `local/work-ahead/training-mech-textures-v1/mechlib-model-uv-usage.json` and its review page. The Training AIV records specify skin ID 7 for instructor roles and ID 2 for target-mech/training-mech roles in missions 2–4; see `local/work-ahead/training-mech-textures-v1/training-skin-ids.json`. The numeric-ID-to-texture-suffix mapping and in-game rendering remain open. These are work-ahead candidates, not integrated replacements.
