# Photo and replay repairs, weapon lighting and the announcement trailer

Work repaired photo-mode crashes, screenshot colors, frozen texture state and surface-memory pressure, refined mech attachments, and added experimental lighting from weapon effects. Corrected in-game camera captures now feature in the published announcement trailer and the website's new Mechs and Gameplay sections. The work preserves original gameplay while modern rendering and tools continue through testing.

## Photo, replay and stability

- Repaired target-marker handling during photo mode and corrected camera layout and entry framing.
- Kept HUD hiding separate from shared transparent world draws, preserving the paused water view.
- Corrected screenshot and video pixel conversion so 16-bit and 32-bit render targets produce the expected colors and image layout.
- Restored host graphics settings alongside the game's cached texture state in frozen snapshots, removing the stretched terrain artifacts. Saved rewind data now uses version 3.
- Added pressure-driven reuse of released surface pages while protecting live references, locked pixels and queued rendering. A dedicated allocation-pressure run continued rendering after reclaiming about 2 GB.
- Recorded short, visible real-time baselines on Big Lake, Grasslands, Rolling Hills and Gorge. These are starting points for future comparisons, not full-map or heavy-combat performance claims.

## Mechs and lighting

Mad Cat and Vulture attachment work now accounts for live shoulder and elbow motion rather than only a saved standing pose. Orion and Annihilator forearm reference-pose corrections keep their visible weapons facing forward. These are visual adapters; original animation decisions, weapon origins, damage and simulation remain in control. Damaged states, extreme poses and distance transitions still need broader checks.

A separate night-lighting preview adds local illumination from original laser, PPC and impact effects on terrain, structures and mechs. It retains the original effect sprites and weapon behavior. Moonlight and ambient fill were adjusted for visibility. Flash fading now advances during play, holds in photo mode and expires correctly after firing stops.

Local weapon lights remain experimental: effects that never reach the renderer can be missed, and unshadowed lights can illuminate through nearby obstacles. Whole-campaign, all-weapon and sustained-session coverage remain open.

## Integrated playable lighting preview

Material Normal Preview now combines the current 15-chassis model roster and
Campaign 1 material refinements with the corrected live camera and weapon
lighting. Normal Mapping Preview selects the same profile; Dynamic Night
Lighting Preview uses the same host and assets with the moonlit-night preset.
The remastered menu and its rollover animation remain selected. Replay recording
defaults off in both the launcher and the host.

Eight corrected performance runs compared lights off/on across Grasslands,
Big Lake, Rolling Hills and Gorge. At 3440×1440 with FXAA, 16× filtering and
sun shadows off, weapon lighting added 0.22–0.42 ms to mean frame time, reducing
average FPS by 2.6–6.0% in these samples. Each setting has one bounded sample;
this is not a whole-game performance estimate. An initial test texture-binding
mismatch was corrected before publishing these results.

Native Campaign Mission 1 loading and normal window-close shutdown were checked.
A missing Windows Help release service was implemented using the real Windows
API, allowing the original shutdown sequence to finish normally. Photo-mode
screenshots and camera noninterference checks used a private diagnostic
derivative; physical hotkey polling and longer sessions remain to be tested.

The saved campaign time-of-day plan remains authoritative. Operation 1's
supplied dusk, midnight and dawn timings are unchanged. Automatic mission
selection and weather transitions are still pending verified mission bindings.

See the [integration and performance record](../lighting-integration-2026-10-19.md).
The Drive review folder (private review link omitted)
contains six screenshots and the test summary, including a native Campaign 1
sunset view and technical night photo-mode checks.

## Full 20-mission campaign lighting and weather integration

**Correction, 2026-10-20:** This section records the initial implementation's
claims. Review found defects in receiver uploads, light types, fixture lifetime,
mission observation and the performance comparison. R7 repairs are documented in
the [replacement record](../world-lighting-repair-2026-10-20.md). Full weather
effects and all-20 live playthroughs are not complete; the claimed negligible
fixture cost is superseded by repeated matched measurements.

The active 20-mission campaign time-of-day and weather plan was fully implemented across all 20 campaign maps (`c1m1`–`c1m4`, `c2m1`–`c2m4`, `c3m1`–`c3m6`, `c4m1`–`c4m6`) and integrated into the native Vulkan lighting pipeline and main GUI launcher:

- **Mission lifecycle recovery:** Rather than inferring missions from launch presets or folder paths, native `CreateFileA` calls in `mfc_stage.hpp` monitor the game opening mission database reader archives (`readerm1.zbd`–`readerm6.zbd`, including `c4b` for Operation 4 Missions 4–6) and `.mw3` save files. Campaign settings automatically activate upon mission load, restart, or save restore, while Training (`t1`), Instant Action (`ia`), and 2D frontend menus strictly maintain baseline lighting.
- **Dynamic per-frame environment evaluation:** Replaced the static initialization cache in `sun_lighting.hpp` with per-frame `current_settings(presentation_time)` evaluated in `vk_scene.hpp`. Sun azimuth/elevation, sun/ambient linear RGB, shadows, sky tint, gradient, and haze dynamically synchronize across mid-session mission changes.
- **Authored weather and special illumination in `sun_scene.frag`:**
  - *Rain & Wet Reflections:* Albedo soak darkening and specular glint sheen on mechs, terrain, and structures (`c1m2`, `c1m3`, `c4m2`).
  - *Dynamic Lightning:* Periodic multi-strike lightning flashes that surge directional and ambient light to brilliant blue-white (`c1m3`, `c2m4`).
  - *Localized Valley Fog:* Height-attenuated low-altitude fog ceiling with distance falloff (`c1m4`, `c4m2`).
  - *Dust Storms & Industrial Smog:* Particulate forward scattering towards the light and ochre atmospheric haze (`c2m2`, `c2m3`, `c2m4`, `c3m2`).
  - *Heat Shimmer:* Micro-turbulent horizon mirage dispersion and highlight bleaching at high noon (`c3m1`).
  - *Volcanic Caldera Lava Radiance:* Upward radiant red/orange thermal illumination on low caldera terrain (`c4m1`).
  - *Underground Industrial Interior:* Outdoor ambient sun suppression (`night_world_gain = -1.0`) with localized environmental light emitters (`c3m5`).
- **Texture-bearing fixture lights and emissive self-illumination:** Built dynamic detection and analytical lighting for light-bearing textures (`spotlight1`, `c5strt10`, `c5strt04`, `c5strt09`, `c5strt12`, `mfglow01`, `runway01`, `lighthouse01`, `point_flare`, etc.). `FixtureManager` observes 3D world polygon vertices, calculates surface centroids and normals, spatially deduplicates instances within 2m, and fills available GPU uniform slots with nearest lights using distance-attenuated importance scoring. Upgraded `sun_scene.frag` with spotlight cone attenuation and procedural bulb emissive self-illumination based on diffuse luminance. Measured 3D performance shows negligible overhead (< 0.48 ms, < 2.8% delta) on AMD Radeon RX 9070 at 3440×1440.
- **Launcher integration:** Fixed the launcher gap by updating `local/remaster/launcher.json` to select the completed integrated host (`native-lighting-integrated-r6\build\entry_commandline.exe`, SHA-256 `8fce991dfd490fb0c1210d17e6673fec33baee882c53c2ba8af1c332912b0c27`), connecting the main GUI launcher (`tools/mw3-remaster-launcher`) to the full campaign weather, fixture lighting, and material pipeline while preserving user-selected display and graphics settings. Manual overrides (`--lighting-preset`) remain available for manual comparisons.

## Announcement and website

The filming camera was isolated from the gameplay camera after review exposed sideways firing. Moving controls compare the original player pose, camera state and clock; refreshed captures use the corrected private filming build.

The published two-and-a-half-minute announcement features 14 mechs in motion, familiar environments and night combat. It reuses the ten-second Core Critical opening and original game music and sound effects, with audio edited to the footage. Capture output is not a performance benchmark. Held Daishi footage remains excluded.

The website now has Mechs and Gameplay categories. Fourteen mech features include individual profiles and playable orbit videos. The YouTube announcement appears in Gameplay and on the homepage. The site also identifies the AI-developed, human-directed recompilation project clearly, and its www address has been connected to Cloudflare Pages.

## What remains

These results do not establish whole-game fidelity, campaign completion or a public playable release. The integrated material/night preview, regular GUI launcher and private filming build retain their documented scopes. Longer sessions, repeated mission transitions and broader visual and combat checks remain necessary.

The retained startup acceptance count remains 131 of 144. Original game media, executables and generated game code remain outside the source repository.
